## Public interfaces and runner

`allocate_search(families, allowance_seconds, evidence)` interprets the allowance
as total seconds for the round. It reserves equal per-family envelopes, including
blocked families. Each family's prospective ordered trial configs provide a
finite measured forecast and the existing M07 target budget. Different methods
may receive different trial counts. Configs are frozen through `register_trial`
and execution is interleaved by proposal index. Shared generic improvements and
development information must match; policy families require all fourteen
features, history, EMA, matched meta information and their own-policy refresh.
These allocation labels alone are not training eligibility.

Extensions are separate explicit rounds with an authenticated family curve that
also appears in the supplied evidence. Advancement uses all comparison rows, so
a settled loser cannot manufacture overlap by comparing only with itself.
Boundary LR, unsettled curves, overlapping uncertainty and missing mechanisms
are retained with reasons. No grid or training budget is silently invented.

`select-finalists --config PATH` has four explicit actions: `allocate`, `search`,
`select`, `disposition`. Allocate/search envelopes contain `families`,
`allowance_seconds`, `evidence`, and immutable `output`. Search uses actual
`TrialSpec`/`run_search_trial` admission. Actual elapsed family time, failures,
pre-launch errors and allowance-skipped trials are retained. A failed scientific
resolution never becomes a bootstrap fallback. The runner's deadline is checked
at existing operation boundaries; it cannot preempt a running kernel. Actual
overruns are reported and reduce remaining opportunity.

The factory reaches the existing matrix, EWGS, TRS, learned-scale, damping,
masking, QAR, stochastic and online MetaQuant adapters. Constructor support is
not numerical/parity/scientific admission. Original-W ownership and H06 logical
normalization/clip/rollback remain shared. Ordinary runs receive their actual
telemetry costs, without unused policy scans. Routing uses its required named
CPU quantizer RNG on fresh states; an incompatible imported parent is refused,
never converted or reseeded. Historical model-only parent import retains its
exact W/auxiliary tensors, cursor, named RNG and parent costs under H07/H08.

Initialized frozen MetaQuant is refused. A genuine trained-theta producer and
its selection authority remain a prerequisite. M11 matched policy learning and
selection use their own existing APIs and endpoint authority; this ordinary
search loop does not pretend to implement or complete that campaign.

## Cost views

`cost_quality_frontier(runs, ledger)` accepts `stream`, `total`, `seconds`, or
`unique`. Runs contain a stable `run_id`, parents, terminal status, physical
events, enclosing wall intervals and optional observed checkpoints. Events
contain a globally qualified `event_id`, kind (`loss`, `gradient_only`, `hvp`),
targets, committed stream targets, raw classifications and source intervals.
Intervals use `[raw-source identity, document identity, start, end)` semantics.

Query/additional-loss/backward can classify one loss; they are never summed as
new passes. EWGS forward and loss-backward share one execution, while actual HVP
and gradient-only executions are separate. Repeated identical identities and
shared ancestors deduplicate; contradictory identities reject. Unique targets
are the global source-offset union. Wall time is an interval union; nested
regions are not added. Independent clocks cannot establish a wall union.

`trial_cost_record` authenticates actual result records. New H06 loss events
retain microbatch IDs, completion/backward-launch status and source offsets.
Query events follow actual sorted scorer order. Failed/unknown partial work
keeps known lower bounds; absent historic physical IDs/offsets remain unknown,
without fabricated reconstruction. Legacy counters remain visible verbatim.
Common engineering costs remain unknown, never zero or a complete project
total. E07 owns global reconciliation across task receipts and raw ancestry.

Curves contain all observed milestones with matched stream/total/wall costs and
best BPB against cumulative search expenditure. Failed attempts charge work
without supplying a winning final quality point. Startup/import/archive and
final publication costs are captured by the enclosing completed attempt receipt;
inner step/scorer regions are not added to it.
