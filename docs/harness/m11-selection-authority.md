# M11 selection authority — fix round 1, 11 October 2026

`select_controller(meta_dev, official_dev)` now accepts rows containing exactly `partition`, a frozen `ControllerArtifact`, and an authenticated `endpoint` reference. Caller-supplied losses, horizons and opportunity labels are not selection authority. The artifact's `training` reference must identify `ralup.meta_selection_eligibility.v1`, not an outer-generation or campaign-result file.

No existing M11 calibration or campaign artifact meets this contract. WP06 remains NEEDS_REVISION; no selected artifact or actual final campaign has been created by this fix. Schema fixtures exercise admission logic only, with temporary authority indexes and generated raw bytes. They are not recorded science, production inputs or permission to relabel synthetic experiments.

The eligibility record must be explicitly `eligible_exploratory`, nonsynthetic, confirmation-ineligible and B0-null. It links one resolved `ralup.matched_meta_training.v1` design and completed candidate45, learnedLR49 and static3 family records. The design binds the current complete source identity, the exact frozen normalizer and corpus, and the indexed M07 exploratory recipe. Its ordered opportunities declare equal ages, horizons, antithetic pair counts, query counts, full14feature/history access and charged own-policy refresh steps. Each family completion links authenticated generation receipts with exact before/after vectors, paired state/input identities and measured update/query counts. Own-policy refresh receipts must bind that family's current unperturbed vector, source, data, design, ages and generated work. All families must share the same ordered training/query input identities. The selected vector must equal its completed lineage.

Normalizer data retains its original META-only trajectory provenance. Selection checks its finite14means/stds, authenticated reference population and corpus binding. It does not refit it, import old complete states or assert future learned-distribution coverage. Recipe authority is read from the repository's M07 index, and corpus authority from its accepted H02 index. The production API has no fixture bypass or caller-provided authority-index argument.

Each endpoint links a prospectively frozen `ralup.selection_endpoint_design.v1`, the exact artifact and eligibility hashes, complete runtime source, seed, partition/corpus, actual query blocks and full continuation. Its producer binds the design before work; completion follows startup. The selector derives the loss from authenticated finite NLL and scored-target counts. Source-scoped document membership, raw article bytes, labels, score masks, duplicates and target counts are checked without running a model. Official development must cover the complete validation partition; META-development may use its declared fixed subset. Protected test documents have no admitted partition.

A `ralup.meta_nomination.v1` record binds the complete META endpoint inventory and computed family nominees. All META endpoints must complete before nomination; nomination must precede each official endpoint design and producer startup. Official rows must contain exactly those nominees, with matched seed/input/full-horizon opportunities. All three families are required in both stages.

These records are a finite admission contract, not a new generic provenance system. A future E01/reopened-WP06 producer must emit truthful completed records from its actual prospectively frozen matched campaign and development endpoints. This fix neither fabricates that producer's measurements nor upgrades the exploratory recipe to accepted B0, production or confirmation. Future whole-state applicability remains strict after the selector source change.
